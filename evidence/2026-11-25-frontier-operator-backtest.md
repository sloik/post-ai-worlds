# Backtest of the frontier operator forecast

**Decision on 25 November 2026:** Keep the 2030 compute-based question as an unscored research indicator. The intended outcomes are one, two, or three-plus independent operators. Today's public evidence can move the observed count between these categories through missing data and estimate choices. Three historical years also provide too little evidence for calibrated odds.

## 2023–2025 results

We screened the [Epoch AI Notable AI Models CSV](https://epoch.ai/data/ai-models-documentation/downloads) retrieved 25 November 2026 (1,102 data rows, SHA-256 `18edeb157374fdea1bf54d84facdde404286d349a736d400db7f09a93e9fa5de`). We kept rows tagged Language, with API, hosted or open-weights access and an empty Base model field. A blank base-model field is a screening convention. For each year and organization, keep the highest numeric Training compute value, group Google and Google DeepMind, and count groups whose value times ten or three reaches the highest value that year. The denominator for missingness includes candidate rows without a numeric value. The date corrections and lineage screen below address boundary cases. The remaining rows have not all been independently adjudicated for public release, family, or ultimate control.

| Year | Screened source rows without a compute estimate | Raw counts ≤10× / ≤3× | After release and lineage checks ≤10× / ≤3× | After also requiring Epoch's Likely or Confident grade ≤10× / ≤3× |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2023 | 12 / 35 | 3 / 2 | 6 / 2 | 5 / 2 |
| 2024 | 26 / 50 | 11 / 5 | 11 / 6 | 8 / 4 |
| 2025 | 43 / 72 | 2 / 2 | 2 / 2 | 2 / 2 |

All counts describe the screened public estimates. They neither establish a lower bound on the true relative count nor imply that every counted organization controlled its compute supply. An omitted larger run could raise the benchmark and remove previously counted groups. An omitted run above the cutoff could add an operator.

### Cases that changed the result

- **2023 publication and release.** Google's launch record puts public Gemini Ultra 1.0 availability on 8 February 2024. Its December technical publication appeared in the 2023 rows and supplied the raw benchmark of about 5.0 × 10²⁵ FLOP. Move that row to 2024. Leave Inflection-2's 2023 release unresolved because its announcement described Pi deployment as forthcoming. This leaves GPT‑4 at 2.1 × 10²⁵ FLOP as the 2023 point-estimate benchmark. Google remains in the 2023 count through PaLM 2. [Google's Gemini Ultra launch](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/bard-gemini-advanced-app/) · [Inflection's archived announcement via republication](https://vuink.com/post/vasyrpgvba-d-dnv/inflection-2) · [OpenAI's GPT‑4 report](https://cdn.openai.com/papers/gpt-4.pdf).
- **2025 pretraining lineage and compute.** The source notes estimate Grok 4 at around 5.0 × 10²⁶ FLOP by assuming shared pretraining with Grok 3 and adding post-training. The latter has a separate estimate around 3.5 × 10²⁶. We exclude the Grok 4 row in the lineage screen under that assumption. GPT‑4.5 then sets the benchmark at 3.8 × 10²⁶, and only OpenAI and xAI clear either threshold. Using the alternative GPT‑4.5 estimate in Epoch's notes (around 2 × 10²⁵) leaves only xAI inside threefold. OpenAI still clears tenfold through the dataset's GPT‑5 estimate. In a diagnostic calculation, lowering the Grok 3 point estimate by 10% as well leaves three groups inside tenfold, including Anthropic through Claude 3.7 Sonnet. The 10% change is a sensitivity check. [xAI Grok 3 release](https://x.ai/news/grok-3) · [xAI Grok 4 release](https://x.ai/news/grok-4) · [Epoch estimation fields](https://epoch.ai/data/ai-models-documentation/records).
- **2025 missing denominator.** Google publicly released Gemini 3 Pro on 18 November. Epoch lists it with no numeric training compute. With the default 3.8 × 10²⁶ FLOP benchmark held fixed, a Google estimate at or above 3.8 × 10²⁵ and no higher than the benchmark would create a third observed group at tenfold, all else equal. A higher Google estimate could also change who else qualifies. The public release confirms a real supplier exists outside the numeric screen. Its missing compute figure makes the category unresolved. [Google's Gemini 3 release](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-3/) · [Epoch model data](https://epoch.ai/data/ai-models).

## Who counts as independent?

Use the entity with final authority over the model's external release at the time of release. Consolidate subsidiaries with their ultimate parent. Identify joint release authority if the public record supports it. Use ownership filings, merger decisions or published contract terms. Record shared cloud and licensing relationships as dependencies, and classify a case as unresolved if a plausible common veto could change the final category and available evidence cannot settle it.

The UK CMA found that Microsoft's 2024 hire of Inflection's core team plus related arrangements, including a non-exclusive IP license, constituted a relevant merger situation. That supports recording a material transfer of development capacity. It does not by itself establish that Microsoft controlled release of Inflection's models. The CMA separately found material influence in the OpenAI partnership without a change to de facto control as of its March 2025 decision. The parties later published clauses on IP and API rights. These show why commercial influence and release control need separate evidence. SpaceX's acquisition of xAI in February 2026 is a clear common-parent case from that date onward. It has no effect on the 2025 historical count. [CMA Inflection decision](https://www.gov.uk/cma-cases/microsoft-slash-inflection-ai-inquiry) · [CMA OpenAI decision](https://www.gov.uk/cma-cases/microsoft-slash-openai-partnership-merger-inquiry) · [Published 2025 contract summary](https://openai.com/index/next-chapter-of-microsoft-openai-partnership/) · [xAI acquisition announcement](https://x.ai/news/xai-joins-spacex).

## Forecast decision

The one/two/three-plus format remains useful. The relative-compute resolver is too sensitive to undisclosed training costs, evidence grades and uncertain lineage to register with odds now. Revisit only if comparable third-party estimates or disclosures can bound the relevant runs and public release control can be determined. The three-year screen is a measurement check, with no three-trial base rate inferred from it.

A revised question could count independent vendors with public models inside a prespecified performance margin on a named third-party benchmark composite, using a dated snapshot and explicit coverage and uncertainty rules. That measures the availability of near-frontier alternatives, while the compute screen can continue to track training capacity. Benchmark coverage and scoring revisions would require a separate validation. Keep the [guardian question](2026-11-25-ai-plurality-and-human-control.md#candidate-forecast-for-the-february-2027-review) separate. Its evidence is an actual intervention under human authority.
