# Proposed measurement protocol: companions and civic dependence

Version 1, 6 November 2026. This resolves the instrument-design part of [issue 4](https://github.com/sloik/post-ai-worlds/issues/4), alongside the [evidence table](companions-and-civic-dependence.md). These are **uncommissioned study designs**, not measured baselines, registered forecasts, data collection or automatic monitoring. No probabilities or event thresholds have been issued. Existing [frozen forecasts](forecasts/2026-11-05-records.json) remain unchanged.

## Common recording rules

Before any study starts, freeze a dated protocol: jurisdiction, eligibility and sampling frame; product/model/version and conversational versus physical exposure; comparison and assignment; primary outcomes and instruments; baseline date; observation window; exclusions; missing-data and attrition handling; source resolution and update policy. Record observation dates separately from publication dates. An unknown baseline stays `not_measured`, never zero. A public-record search that finds nothing stays `not_found_in_search`, never proof that no activity occurred.

Report the eligible, approached, enrolled and retained denominators, uncertainty and selection limits. Predefine a smallest effect of interest and analysis before seeing outcomes; report nulls and adverse results. Separate assigned treatment from voluntary usage, intention-to-treat from adherence, and within-person changes from population differences. Multiple articles on the same sample are one lineage. Record commercial sponsorship and author/provider interests. None of this makes an observational study causal.

The following windows are proposed planning choices. If commissioning occurs later, version the design prospectively; do not backdate a missed baseline. A forecast requires separate [forecast registration](../docs/forecasting-guide.md), including odds and resolution terms. TLA+ could guard state/identity rules, not establish psychological, demographic or political effects.

## Candidate instruments

| ID and question | Proposed population, instrument and window | Dated baseline and exclusions | Missing-data rule and interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| SOC-01 — Does conversational companionship support offline connection? | Consenting UK-resident adults 18+ in a declared panel frame; baseline then monthly for January–December 2027. Record product/version, exposure, validated loneliness scale, offline contact/functioning and problematic-use measure; freeze exact scale editions and scoring before enrollment. Analyse vulnerability and prior social network as prespecified moderators. | `not_measured` as of 2026-11-06. Proposed baseline before first exposure measurement in January 2027. Distinguish prior users/new users/nonusers; exclude invalid/duplicate records under rules frozen before outcomes; do not discard distressed or high-use participants to improve results. | Report attrition by group and baseline vulnerability; no zero-fill or last-observation-carried-forward. Prespecify a justified missingness model and sensitivity to nonrandom dropout. Voluntary use remains observational; an ethically approved assigned comparison would need a separate protocol. No physical-companion or youth inference. |
| SOC-02 — What evidence exists about youth development? | Review publicly available primary studies with participants under 18, companion definition and offline developmental/relationship outcomes. Search a frozen database/query list quarterly during 2027; record age bands, design, follow-up and safety reporting. | Literature baseline `not_measured`; freeze a reproducible search on 2027-01-01 before prospective updates. Exclude adult-only studies from youth effect estimates, while retaining them as explicitly indirect evidence. No recruitment of minors is commissioned. | Report inaccessible studies and missing ages/outcomes as unknown; do not infer safety from absence of reports. Group multiple publications from one cohort. A study's existence and its result are separate observations; no pooled estimate without compatible designs. |
| SOC-03 — Does use affect wanted partnership or births? | Proposed UK adult panel aged 20–44, stratified by baseline partnership, parenting intentions and existing children; January 2027–December 2028. Record product/exposure separately from partnership transitions, wanted family size, intentions and actual births, plus housing, income, care access and health covariates. | `not_measured` as of review date; prospective January 2027 baseline. Exclude chatbot adoption inferred solely from country-level sales; retain people who change products or stop use. Define handling of pregnancy already underway at baseline. | Report item nonresponse separately for each sensitive variable; use aggregate consented outcomes, no intimate transcripts. Attrition and residual confounding may prevent an effect estimate. Intentions are not births; national fertility trends are not an AI counterfactual. A two-year panel cannot establish completed lifetime fertility. |
| SOC-04 — How widespread is synthetic intimate-media abuse? | Repeated, comparably sampled UK adult surveys during 2027 with identical consent/likeness definitions; combine separately with public aggregate platform/help-service reports. Measure reported victimisation, perpetration, help-seeking and removal experiences, distinguishing generated imagery, altered imagery and consensual expression. | `not_measured` for this instrument. Existing published surveys are historical estimates for their samples, not a 2027 baseline. Freeze baseline survey in January 2027. Exclude duplicate administrative records only with a documented method; never request or retain abuse images. | Survey nonresponse, recall, disclosure and platform detection differ. Report channels separately; do not add reports and survey prevalence as if disjoint people. Missing complaints do not establish no abuse, and findings about abuse do not estimate all adult-media use or its net relationship effects. |
| SOC-05 — Can a public institution actually leave a provider? | Freeze a list of UK local authorities and consequential AI-supported service contracts publicly identifiable on 2027-01-01; this is a cohort of publicly identifiable AI-service contracts, not a prevalence sample of all local-authority dependence; document per-contract critical functions, data export, operating expertise, substitute availability, migration cost/time and tested fallback through December 2027. | No current audit baseline. A replacement must preserve the defined consequential function at disclosed cost/quality; a procurement announcement or another reseller of the same upstream provider does not establish independence. Exclude ordinary software without a disclosed AI-dependent function, with reasons. | Distinguish confidential, not published, denied and absent records. No missing record counts as no fallback. Track upstream dependencies; evidence on one service does not establish government-wide sovereignty or firm dominance. |
| SOC-06 — Has public control been exercised? | In the same frozen service cohort/window, record all eligible dated appeals, audits, regulator/court processes and supplier-replacement attempts, including rejected, unsuccessful and pending cases. Record who had standing, authority and remedy implementation; measure an actual operating-service change separately from an attempted or ordered remedy. | `not_measured`; January 2027 review must distinguish pre-existing powers, pending proceedings and implemented remedies. Exclude a policy promise alone from the exercised-control numerator, retaining it as a separate category. | Pending, unpublished and unverified outcomes remain unresolved. Publish the searched sources and denominator, without identifying affected service users. An implemented remedy supports scoped contestability, not general democratic legitimacy. |
| SOC-07 — Can a smaller independent model do a useful civic task? | Proposed preregistered benchmark for public procurement-document extraction and source-grounded claim checking. Freeze task set and held-out documents before evaluation in Q1 2027; compare human-only, independently runnable smaller-model assistance and a disclosed frontier-service comparator under matched budgets. | No benchmark baseline or performance threshold measured. Define “smaller” relative to the named comparator (model identity/size where disclosed, serving hardware, resource and licence budget), correctness/critical errors, blinded scoring, review effort, elapsed time and full operating cost before execution. Hold out supplier variants and document duplicates; separate training-like material. | Report every task failure, timeout and unscorable item; include failures in cost/reliability denominators. A runnable model with remote OCR, storage or inference still has dependencies. Task competence does not grant funds, assets, trusted membership, legal standing or political mandate. |
| SOC-08 — Does citizen capability become durable collective leverage? | Freeze a publicly documented cohort of member-governed civic organisations using AI by 2027-01-01; quarterly follow-up for 12 months on useful verified output, membership decision rights, asset/data ownership, funding continuity, audience access, enforceable standing and provider exit exercises. | `not_measured`. Incorporation/demo/available weights are eligibility context, not success. Define sustained output and independent verification before observation; record abandoned organisations rather than dropping them. | Separate unknown/private financial or membership records from negative findings. Small, self-selected cases cannot yield a population success probability. Improvements may come from staff/funding/institutions rather than AI; compare alternatives before attributing gains. |

## How the mechanisms can differ

A positive SOC-07 result supports task sufficiency while SOC-05 can still show infrastructure dependence. Better immediate mood can coexist with unchanged offline contact or more dependency in SOC-01. Helpful assistance may improve conditions for wanted children while partnership substitution works in the other direction; SOC-03 has to measure both pathways. SOC-06 records a remedy, not a moral proof of representation. These combinations are coherent, rather than contradictions to remove by choosing one preferred story.

No instrument treats access to personal AI as sufficient for citizen counterbalance. Assets, funding, trusted association, communication channels, practical exit and enforceable standing are distinct inputs. A failure to observe any one of them is a question for investigation, not permission to infer authoritarian control or prescribe confrontation.
