# Companions and civic dependence: scoped evidence table

Version 1, researched 6 November 2026. This addresses [issue 4](https://github.com/sloik/post-ai-worlds/issues/4) with thirteen primary evidence lineages and [eight proposed measurement protocols](social-measurement-protocol.md). It is a scoped review, not a systematic review or a count of independent confirmations. Rows retain sample/design/version, observation window, opposing evidence and missingness. Publication and observation dates differ; unspecified details remain unknown.

Adult conversational systems, generated media, youth exposure, hypothetical capable physical companions and public institutions are different populations and mechanisms. No row tests capable intimate robots or estimates AI-caused fertility change. An empirical report, a primary project summary and a policy analysis also carry different evidential weight. The final column makes those distinctions explicit.

## What these sources leave open

Immediate relief, offline integration, dependency and developmental effects are different outcomes. Published adult conversational results cannot fill the embodied-companion or youth-development gaps. Synthetic intimate-media abuse is a demonstrated research topic, while the net effects of consensual synthetic adult media on long-term relationships remain unresolved in this scoped table. No included source estimates the net causal effect of sex robots on fertility, family formation or population.

The civic studies support particular assistance, mediation and persuasion mechanisms. Durable counterbalance additionally needs independently usable infrastructure, productive assets, sustained funding, trusted association, audience access, and enforceable standing. A smaller model might be sufficient for one externally verifiable task; none of these sources establishes that this alone equalises political power. A competent provider or public institution still needs a separately justified mandate.

Use the [eight proposed measurement protocols](social-measurement-protocol.md) to make the missing comparisons explicit. Baselines remain `not_measured`; no numerical forecast or monitoring programme is registered. The associated [worlds](../worlds/README.md) distinguish concentrated dependence from useful capability without claiming either outcome is inevitable.

## Access boundary and update rule

Relevant full methods/discussion were inspected for INT-01–05 and INT-08; INT-06 is explicitly a primary project summary with unresolved method fields and INT-07 a policy analysis. CIV-02 retains the publisher/author-record access boundary. Additional 2026 Nature Human Behaviour and human-peer/chatbot comparison leads were not admitted as fully verified rows where full methods were inaccessible. This is not a claim that no additional or later evidence exists. New findings should add a source/version and limitations rather than silently overwrite the meaning of an old row.

AI-assisted research and drafting were checked against public primary abstracts, methods and official reports where noted. No raw participant conversations, abuse images or private source material are included.
