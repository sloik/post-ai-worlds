# Evidence and forecasts

Public chapters cite public sources directly. A source establishes only what it actually reports. A vendor announcement, interview, independent experiment and population study carry different evidential weight. Repeated appearances by one witness are a shared source lineage, not independent confirmations.

Distinguish observations, candidate indicators, forecasts, conditional arguments and values. Unknown or unmeasured baselines are not negative results. A plausible mechanism is not an estimated effect size. Laboratory and self-reported findings do not establish deployment performance or society-wide outcomes.

## Signals worth developing

Track capability and concentration separately. Candidate indicators include practical model portability, switching cost, independent access to compute and energy, productive ownership, labour bargaining power, enforceable public oversight, synthetic-companion use, displacement of human interaction, AI-welfare advocacy, and cultural expression around automation. Each needs a population, measurement instrument, sampling limits and a dated baseline before prospective thresholds can be registered.

For intimate AI and youth development, distinguish adult choice from adolescent exposure, frequency from dependency, and correlation from causation. Longitudinal evidence is needed to estimate relationship or demographic effects. A viral song can show a cultural motif; it cannot estimate public belief or job loss.

## Forecast discipline

Prediction submissions must meet the [superforecasting-style contribution standard](../docs/forecasting-guide.md). Prefer our public skill/tool; equivalent manual analysis is accepted. Numerical belief, resolution terms, outside-view rationale, clashing cases, dependence disclosure and update/scoring plans are required. TLA+ verification is encouraged but optional, and its absence is never an acceptance blocker. Question-design ideas without odds remain ideas.


The [frozen public forecast export](forecasts/2026-11-05-records.json) is now retained in this canonical repository; the [public Observatory](https://stocki-apps.pages.dev/omnicorp/observatory/) presents it. The export preserves the original issued records byte for byte. Linking here does not re-register their dates or alter probabilities, criteria, exclusions or observation windows. Read the original record before judging its outcome. This repository's fresh history is not evidence of an earlier public preregistration.

New proposals need a precise event, issue date, deadline, resolution source, exclusions, treatment of missing data and supporting/opposing cases. Label subjective probabilities and justify them. A forecast issue remains a proposal until a maintainer explicitly records it. Later corrections append a dated revision rather than silently rewriting the original question.

## Submit evidence

Use the evidence issue form with the claim/chapter/OMNI IDs, direct source URL and date, what it supports and challenges, whether the source is primary, and limitations or shared lineage. For contradictions, distinguish a logical conflict under the same scope from disagreement about evidence, values or model encoding.

Only public links and original limited annotations belong here. Do not upload copied books, articles, transcripts, paid-source material, personal records or raw operational receipts. Public visibility of an isolated source does not grant rights to publish a private compiled framework.
