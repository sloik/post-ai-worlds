# What would turn faster AI research into a fast takeoff?

**Reviewed 27 November 2026.** Ege Erdil and Daniel Kokotajlo agree that the chance of a rapid transition warrants preparation. They disagree about how quickly AI can replace the research process and how far faster research can carry capabilities before it runs into experiment, oversight or deployment constraints. Erdil puts a software-only explosion over the next three years at roughly 10–20%. Kokotajlo puts full AI research automation in 2027 slightly above even odds absent substantial slowdown, and expects a fast feedback loop to follow in many of those cases. The host summarises Kokotajlo’s broader near-term superintelligence view as above 50%. These are attributed judgments with different conditions and endpoints. **Q01 method.** Start from the existing 70% estimate, which already counted the initial Alpöge report and discounted it for the then-unfinished checks. Treat the new correctness checks as one correlated bundle and apply a subjective 1.12× multiplier to 2.33 prior odds, yielding 72.3%, rounded to 72%. Multipliers of 1.04–1.20 yield 70.8–73.7%. For the revision, independent checks reduce the chance that a major reported AI-assisted result disappears on replay. Against a larger revision, correctness is only one gate and the same mechanism and team are being checked repeatedly. The probability could be too high if the contribution trace shows that people supplied the crucial method, or too low if a separate team releases a qualifying result in a second domain. The original ensemble was not re-elicited. Record the revision in the existing Q01 probability history for later Brier scoring. The observation window and the resolution rules are unchanged. ## Follow up on 5 February 2027

- **Research automation.** Track three observable steps: an agent owns a whole research question through a reviewable result, an unaffiliated team reproduces a model improvement at the stated budget, and useful gains recur on successive checkpoints. Request enough lineage to distinguish who framed and selected the hypothesis, who ran the experiments, and who judged the result. Compare accepted improvements, accelerator-hours, wall time, review and rework against a human-led baseline. For Q01, seek proof contribution provenance, expert novelty and correspondence review, and a second unaffiliated domain. [Research proxy criteria](forecasts/2026-11-13-rsi-forecasts.md)
- **Physical and digital transfer.** For laundry, track accepted garments per staffed hour, quality, interventions and resets on unseen mixed loads in unfamiliar homes, with the same human comparison. For games, a held-out release sample, ordinary inputs, unattended completion and a predefined time budget would distinguish gameplay transfer from game-specific preparation. Treat both as monitoring designs until a baseline, sampling frame and resolver are fixed.
- **Effective oversight.** Look for actual compute-allocation attestations, their coverage and who can verify them. Track an independent authority’s ability to pause and roll back a deployment while preserving service and appeal. For Omnicorp, compare verification cost and false positives when the same class of proposal is tested at cheap screening scale and independent acceptance scale.
