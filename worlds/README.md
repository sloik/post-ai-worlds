# Shared numbered worlds

Post-AI Era Vision asks about possible societies. Omnicorp asks what an organisation could do within them. Both use [one register](registry.json). A number identifies a declared contract; it is not a probability, moral ranking, prediction or an exclusive global timeline.

These bundles describe different dimensions. A country may combine some public capacity with concentrated private infrastructure; a sector may have abundant digital supply and scarce physical inputs. Combining IDs does not merge their premises or certify compatibility. Scope each comparison by domain, population and horizon before checking it. Add a versioned branch when a genuinely distinct contract is needed; preserve old identity and lineage.

## What a record means

An **observation** is a dated report with provenance and limits. An **assumption** is a premise to test or adopt conditionally. A **conditional consequence** depends on those premises and an argument or model. A **normative decision** states a value or chosen rule, not a discovered fact. An **operational design** needs implementation evidence. A **mandate** is authorised power within a defined process and scope; capacity or usefulness alone does not supply it.

The register holds 29 assumptions and 24 selected editorial qualifications with stable ASM/CLM IDs. A claim's `universe_ids` express relevance. They do not admit that claim into every named branch. `admitted_claim_ids` begin empty. A declared admission must supply the claim's premises, and even that is a consistency condition rather than evidence that the premises hold in society.

The public edition omits private source excerpts. CLM-007 is conditional, CLM-011 points to separately registered forecasts, and CLM-023/024 are version-2 general qualifications about workload-dependent planning and review. No old empirical source/proof status is silently carried across. Universe assurance remains `unchecked`.

## Coverage and gaps

[Mapping](mapping.json) gives each public chapter a conceptual relevance disposition and a limit. Alignment failure modes, cosmic horizons, developmental effects and many institutional mechanisms are **not encoded** by the current ten worlds. An explicit gap is preferable to forcing an unrelated chapter into a false formal branch. [Four separately numbered proposal contracts](proposed-branch-contracts.md) now define alignment/evasion, useful containment, lifecycle resources and delayed authority. They remain proposed and not modelled, outside the ten registered universes; no admission or assurance transfers.

[Coverage](coverage.json) records exact public file bytes and actual ATX heading locations outside code fences. Every heading is inventoried; sentences are not automatically interpreted or proved. Run `python3 tools/update_inventory.py` to update it. This is an explicit editorial baseline change, reviewed in the same PR as the text; update mapping for new or moved chapters. There is no dependency on another repository's history or a private baseline.

[Selected finite models](../formal/README.md) check premise admission/versioning and small composed operational contracts. Native source/model hashes apply to those exact abstractions. They do not extend formal assurance to either reading track.

For a substantive change: identify the affected premise/claim; state domain and horizon; record opposing evidence; update relevance and coverage; rerun affected models if model inputs change. Use an [issue or PR](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Worlds retain disagreements as explicit alternatives rather than editing them into false consensus.

The vision's era A/B/C, work branches A–D, and alignment branches A–E are local editorial labels. None is an alias for an OMNI ID. Legacy U1–U4 map only to the original institutional bundles OMNI-101–104.
