# Reading Omnicorp through the shared worlds

The [shared worlds](../worlds/README.md) make assumptions explicit across the societal and organisational reading tracks. They preserve stable `OMNI-*` identities without implying a ranking, an arrival order or a probability. A world may concern one task, sector or jurisdiction rather than the whole planet.

## The numbered settings

| ID | Conceptual setting |
|---|---|
| `OMNI-000` | Evidence reference: dated observations and reported results, without treating every source as true |
| `OMNI-001` | Founder bootstrap: a bounded organisation developing useful capabilities under explicit authority |
| `OMNI-101` | Dispersed capability and ownership: practical alternatives can be operated independently |
| `OMNI-102` | Dependent users: useful assistants coexist with concentrated services or expensive exit |
| `OMNI-103` | Accountable public capacity: institutions retain usable expertise, scrutiny, remedies and alternatives |
| `OMNI-104` | Concentrated control: providers or authorities restrict meaningful alternatives or association |
| `OMNI-201` | Material abundance with plural ownership: productive expansion and practical alternatives coexist |
| `OMNI-202` | Material abundance with concentrated control: plentiful output does not remove dependence |
| `OMNI-301` | Bounded adaptive collective: accepted improvement depends on resources, evaluation and continuity |
| `OMNI-401` | Relevant plateau: capability or accessible resources do not improve enough for a specified next step |

These settings can overlap across sectors. Their labels are not an exhaustive event partition whose probabilities should add to one. Intimacy, cultural preference and public legitimacy are additional dimensions, not automatic consequences of a favourable capability branch.

## What document coverage means

A public inventory can establish which documents and headings were enrolled and whether their source identities still match. It cannot establish that every sentence has been decomposed into an atomic claim or that every interpretation has been checked.

Classify source reports, assumptions, conditional mechanisms, commitments and operating designs separately. A normative commitment does not become an empirical law when stored in machine-readable form. A well-formed source reference establishes where a statement appears, not whether its inference is sound.

## What formal assurance means

Consult the shared register's current public assurance route for the exact models, properties, inputs and observed results. Results about earlier or different inputs do not automatically apply to this edition. Do not copy a pass label across a rewritten registry, a changed premise or a new implementation.

Selected formal properties may guard claim admission, evidence freshness, authority or exit. Other obligations remain empirical, normative or open. It is useful for a model to reject an unsupported transfer without proving that either world is likely.

Contributors should name the setting they mean, identify the assumption they challenge and supply the observation or argument that would improve the decision. A proposed additional world earns its place by clarifying a substantive difference, not by multiplying labels. The organisation's cornerstone remains [continual development](13-CONTINUAL-SELF-DEVELOPMENT.md); the register helps make that development more explicit and correctable.
