# Candidate social signals

This is a proposed manual research register for the questions in [culture, intimacy and power](15-CULTURE-INTIMACY-AND-POWER.md). It contains no measured baseline, registered probability or automatic monitoring programme. The point is to identify observations that could change a scenario argument before collecting an impressive but uninformative dashboard.

The [scoped evidence table](../evidence/companions-and-civic-dependence.md) now records thirteen source lineages, opposing findings and design limits. [Eight measurement protocols](../evidence/social-measurement-protocol.md) specify proposed populations/windows, baseline status, exclusions and missing-data handling. They are uncommissioned designs, not measured baselines, registered odds or monitoring.

## Eight questions worth instrumenting

| Candidate | What to look for | What it would not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Cultural organisation | A named community with continuing activity and a demand addressed to an institution | A popular song alone does not establish a durable constituency or representative opinion |
| AI-welfare institutions | A preservation or treatment commitment with an identifiable enforcement mechanism | Advocacy or a laboratory promise does not establish consciousness or enforceable rights |
| Human connection | Longitudinal or experimental work measuring offline relationships and functioning alongside companion use | Correlation, short adult exposure and a result for text systems cannot establish all developmental or embodied effects |
| Family formation | Suitable evidence linking adoption patterns with partnership, intentions and actual births, with confounders addressed | National fertility trends alone cannot identify an intimate-AI effect |
| Public-service dependence | A documented consequential service without a practicable independent fallback or supplier substitution | A large procurement contract alone does not establish lost sovereignty |
| Exercised public control | An actual audit, appeal or supplier replacement that changes a consequential service | An announced oversight body or unused contract clause does not demonstrate effective control |
| Independent task capability | Reproducible useful work with disclosed model, hardware, full costs and external dependencies | Available weights do not establish affordable serving, security, legal permission or productive independence |
| Citizen-owned production | Sustained useful output with accountable member governance, asset ownership and practical alternatives | Incorporation, a demonstration or AI assistance alone does not show sustainable collective power |

These candidates are deliberately narrower than a claim that society is becoming an AI democracy or a corporate state. Different observations may support different mechanisms in the same place.

## Establish scope before resolution

A commissioned review should define a population, jurisdiction, task, observation period, qualifying evidence and limits. Record both publication and observation dates. Separate a missing public record from evidence that the underlying activity is absent. Protect privacy rather than assuming intimate or civic data should be collected because it would be informative.

Study design matters. Selection, attrition, product definitions and baseline vulnerability can change interpretation. Report what the comparison permits before assigning a direction to the result. Evidence of deployment is different from evidence of benefit; evidence of benefit is different from justified authority.

## Turn a candidate into a forecast carefully

A future question needs frozen criteria and a deadline before a probability. Check the current baseline so an existing event cannot masquerade as a prediction. Keep the appearance of a study separate from the direction or size of its estimated effect.

Use [world identifiers](../worlds/README.md) to record which premise an observation bears on. A cheaper model may support task sufficiency without changing ownership; a successful appeal may support one institution without proving general democratic control. Review the instrument when it stops discriminating between the explanations that matter.
