# Evidence and forecasts

An organisation that wants to improve should be able to distinguish what happened from what it expected. A convincing demonstration, a laboratory report, an independent replication and a deployment outcome answer different questions. None becomes a prediction merely because it later appears in a timeline.

This public edition does not reconstruct or re-register earlier forecast records. The [frozen public forecast export](../evidence/forecasts/2026-11-05-records.json) is retained in this canonical repository and presented by the [public Observatory](https://stocki-apps.pages.dev/omnicorp/observatory/). Its original issued bytes are preserved. Each original registration owns its wording, criteria, probability, observation window and resolution rules. A new explanation here must not silently alter those terms or imply that a past event was forecast prospectively.

## Record the narrow observation

For an evidence entry, retain a primary source, publication date, observation date and the precise claim it supports. Say whether the result is reported by its producer, examined externally or reproduced under independent conditions. Related articles repeating one result share an evidence lineage; they are not independent replications.

State the object being improved. A better benchmark score, a useful scientific result, a revised model checkpoint and an organisation learning across tasks are different outcomes. Evidence about one can motivate a question about another without establishing it.

Formal acceptance also has a correspondence problem. A checker can accept a precise statement while that statement differs from the claim people believe was proved. Evaluation should examine the translation between intended claim and checked object, alongside the checker result.

## Define a forecast before assigning odds

A prospective question needs a future deadline, qualifying evidence, exclusions and a missing-data outcome. Conditional questions also need a rule for an antecedent that never occurs. Establish the current baseline so an already realised event cannot be scored as a new prediction.

Probability should follow supporting and opposing cases, dependencies and an explicit account of uncertainty. A precise number without a credible reference class is still a subjective judgment. Its precision should not be confused with calibration.

## Update visibly

Keep the original estimate when evidence changes the assessment. Record the new information, revised judgment, reasoning and what would reverse the update. If a question is defective, supersede it visibly rather than repairing its criteria after seeing the outcome.

A [world identifier](../worlds/README.md) supplies a conditional setting, not a probability. Formal consistency can help reveal incompatible assumptions; it cannot supply the frequency of a social event. The intended discipline is to make observations, forecasts and scenario arguments independently reviewable, including when the organisation's preferred story becomes less plausible.
