# From a small operation to multiple capabilities

A small starting team is a useful discipline: it makes priorities and dependency visible. It is not an end in itself. Reducing headcount can raise a ratio while weakening delivery, resilience or judgment. Omnicorp should expand only when a wider scope has a credible purpose and the resources to support it.

The stages below are proposed gates rather than a promised calendar. An organisation may remain at one stage, reverse direction or decide that an additional business line is a distraction from its purpose.

## Establish one useful capability

Begin with a narrow recurring problem. Identify the beneficiary, the existing alternative and the consequence of an incorrect result. Produce a bounded artifact and observe whether it helps. A successful demonstration establishes that a task can be completed under those conditions; it does not establish demand, reliable operation or profitable delivery.

Before calling the capability a business engine, distinguish willingness to try it from willingness to pay for it repeatedly. Record review effort, support and exceptional cases alongside generation cost. The first process should be simple enough that its owners can understand why it works and notice when it stops working.

## Make the process repeatable

The next step is repeatability without constant improvisation. Instructions, examples, tests and records should allow another authorised operator to reproduce the useful result. An absence or supplier interruption should reveal a planned fallback rather than an undocumented personal dependency.

Research on ML system debt identifies risks from dependencies, feedback loops and changes outside the model itself. It is a reason to evaluate the surrounding system, not just the newest model checkpoint. [Sculley and colleagues](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2015/hash/86df7dcfd896fcaf2674f757a2463eba-Abstract.html).

## Add an adjacent capability

A second engine should exploit a genuine shared asset: knowledge of a customer problem, a tested operating method or a distribution relationship. A familiar model API is not enough. Ask what becomes easier because the first engine exists, and what new risks are introduced by combining them.

Shared resources need allocation rules. Otherwise the successful engine can quietly subsidise an unbounded experiment while both appear busy. Track the new activity's resource use and contribution separately before claiming a beneficial feedback loop.

## Become an organisation that can expand

The longer-term objective is a repeatable ability to enter a domain, learn its constraints, build a bounded operation and decide whether to continue. Some domains require people, assets, licences or elapsed testing time that the original software operation did not need. Expansion should preserve the ability to stop and recover.

`OMNI-001` describes the bootstrap setting; other [worlds](../worlds/README.md) vary access, ownership and resource constraints. No stage transition proves that an organisation can do everything. The useful achievement is a demonstrated next capability with a clear owner and an honest account of its limits.
