# Public sources and how to read them

This is a curated entry point, not a transcript collection or a certification that every claim made by a source is correct. Sources serve different purposes. A policy document states a proposal or rule; an experiment supports a scoped empirical result; a fictional world supplies possibilities rather than observations.

## Capability, deployment and economics

- [*Situational Awareness*, 2024](https://situational-awareness.ai/): an author's public argument about capability, industrial mobilisation and strategic competition. Read its expectations as dated claims, not a consensus timetable.
- [*AI 2027*](https://ai-2027.com/): a detailed scenario with alternative endings. Useful for examining causal chains; narrative detail does not make every premise independently probable.
- [*AI 2040: Plan A*](https://ai-2040.com/): a recommended policy path. Its distinction between desired intervention and expected consequences matters when assessing success.
- [NBER productivity-paradox paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24001): a basis for studying complementary organisational change, not a fixed AI deployment delay.
- [IEA, *Energy and AI*](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai): modelling and evidence on electricity and data centres. National totals should not substitute for local delivery constraints.
- [OECD infrastructure competition study](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/competition-in-artificial-intelligence-infrastructure_623d1874-en.html): supply-chain structure and competition questions. Concentration is a mechanism to investigate, not proof of inevitable corporate sovereignty.

## Measurement, institutions and people

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework): lifecycle and context for risk management; not a certificate of safety.
- [Harvard/BCG jagged-frontier research](https://aiinstitute.hbs.edu/navigating-the-jagged-technological-frontier/): task-specific evidence about assistance, illustrating why nearby tasks can behave differently.
- [ACL contamination report](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.30/): empirical work on benchmark exposure. Its results should not become a claim that every evaluation is useless.
- [UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights/): a normative reference for dignity and participation, not evidence that all existing states realise those principles.
- [OECD deliberative institutions report](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2020/06/innovative-citizen-participation-and-new-democratic-institutions_11aa2baf.html): documented participation practices; AI-mediated improvement remains a separate question.
- [Ryan and Deci, 2000](https://www.selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2000_RyanDeci_SDT.pdf): a psychological account of autonomy, competence and relatedness, not a forecast of life after automation.
- [Banks, *A Few Notes on the Culture*](https://teagmhail.github.io/the-culture/essays/a-few-notes/): the author's fictional institutional setting, valuable for asking which freedoms abundance should support.

## Contributing a source

Give the original publication date, relevant section and narrow claim supported. Separate the authors' results from your interpretation. Include contrary findings and disclose when several reports repeat one source. Avoid depositing copyrighted transcripts or personal data. A better citation should improve an argument, not merely decorate it.
