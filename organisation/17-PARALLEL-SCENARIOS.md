# Coherent parallel scenarios

Parallel scenarios let an argument keep its assumptions visible. Instead of combining whichever features make a preferred conclusion attractive, describe a versioned setting and ask what follows within it. The metaphor of separate worlds is a reasoning aid, not a claim that a model contains complete alternative societies.

A coherent scenario can be undesirable or unlikely. An apparent contradiction may expose an imprecise scope rather than an impossible future. Keep logical coherence, empirical support, normative desirability and probability as different assessments.

## A branch as a contract

Each proposed record needs an identity, version, scope, premises, permitted changes, claims and evidence links. Record why it forked and which earlier assessments became stale. Scope includes the task, population, jurisdiction and interval where those distinctions matter.

Material abundance can coexist with finite physical constraints. Plural suppliers can coexist with concentrated control. Improvement on one task can coexist with plateau on another. Treat assumptions as incompatible only when their meanings and scopes justify that relationship.

A claim relevant to several worlds is not necessarily valid in each. Admission requires the target world's premises to support the claim's stated preconditions. Adding a missing premise is a visible revision, not a silent consequence of copying the claim.

## Use formal tools for bounded questions

TLA+ describes state-based behaviours; its tools can examine precisely specified systems. The official introduction also distinguishes possible steps from fairness conditions requiring progress. It is a useful basis for narrow models of authority, evidence or exit, not a demographic forecasting engine. [Lamport's introduction](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/tla/high-level-view.html).

A candidate model might examine whether capability alone can grant authority, whether expansion requires named evidence gates, or whether an exit advertised as independent still needs a former supplier's approval. The encoded events remain abstractions. A flag called public authorisation does not certify a real institution's legitimacy.

Require a reachable successful example and deliberate adverse controls. Several separately passing models can still conflict when combined. Check overlapping contracts together and investigate whether their combination loses a legitimate successful path. A syntax error or a failure of basic type constraints is not the intended semantic counterexample.

## Preserve the proof boundary

A finite check establishes a result for the encoded model, configuration and bounds. It does not establish that the premises describe society, that every sentence was modelled or that an implementation conforms. Conditional eventual exit also differs from exit merely being reachable and from an unconditional real-world guarantee.

The [shared worlds](../worlds/README.md) own stable `OMNI-*` identities and the route to current public assurance documentation. This chapter makes no blanket pass claim. Empirical studies, institutional observation and independently reviewed translations between prose and model remain necessary even when the bookkeeping is precise.
