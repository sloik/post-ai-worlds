# Post-AI Worlds

An English research project about possible AI futures and the organisations that could act within them.

Two questions belong together: **what might society become, and how should an AI-native organisation develop responsibly inside it?** The societal track explores work, resources, relationships, institutions and power. The organisational track develops Omnicorp: a company whose purpose is to continually improve its ability to understand and act, sustained and tested by useful activity in the world.

This is a collection of arguments to inspect and improve. Evidence, assumptions, conditional consequences, values and operating decisions carry different kinds of authority. A coherent scenario is not automatically likely, desirable or empirically supported.

## One shared world register

Both tracks refer to the same numbered OMNI scenarios. Numbers identify assumption bundles; they do not rank worlds or assign probabilities. Scenarios can overlap across countries, sectors and time horizons.

Document coverage, conceptual relevance and formal assurance are separate. A chapter can discuss a world without every sentence being encoded or proved. A successful finite model check establishes only the recorded property under its stated assumptions and bounds. It does not establish that society follows the model.
