# One maintained source

The canonical home for **both Post-AI Era Vision and Omnicorp** is https://github.com/sloik/post-ai-worlds .

- [post-ai/](post-ai/README.md): societal vision and its arguments.
- [omnicorp/](omnicorp/README.md): the organisation, development, production and institutional questions.
- [worlds/](worlds/README.md): one shared register, assumption identities and document dispositions.
- [formal/](formal/README.md): exact selected bounded assurance.
- [website/](website/README.md): reviewed public introduction and its source, not an alternative doctrine.

Make future substantive changes here, through issues and PRs. Website summaries link here and should be updated when their owning argument changes. Evidence/forecast records retain their original issuance and resolution rules; relocating a public record does not re-register it.

The folders were originally named `vision/` and `organisation/`; the latter hid the Omnicorp name. They are now `post-ai/` and `omnicorp/`. Their Git history is retained. Older private project notes are historical archives and may contain material deliberately excluded from publication. They are not a second maintained source or a way to bypass the public contribution process. Raw private research and immutable old proof receipts remain private historical evidence, not missing public chapters or transferable proof.

This repository's public source identities govern its current checks. Historical hashes and links in old commits, issued records or archives retain their original meaning; do not rewrite them to make an old result appear to cover new bytes. Chapter mapping remains conceptual relevance, not full prose proof.
