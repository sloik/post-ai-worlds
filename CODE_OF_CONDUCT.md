# Code of Conduct

We welcome serious disagreement about AI futures, institutions and the project's own assumptions. Make the argument inspectable and treat the people involved with respect.

Expected conduct:

- Critique claims, methods and decisions without personal attacks or harassment.
- Describe uncertainty honestly and correct material mistakes.
- Respect privacy, copyright and confidentiality.
- Avoid impersonation, fabricated sources, spam and attempts to manufacture consensus.
- Keep public contributions and discussion in English so participants can review a common record.

AI-assisted participation follows the same expectations. Flooding a discussion with generated text or accounts is not a substitute for evidence or engagement with criticism.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock discussions, restrict participation or report platform abuse. Where possible, explain moderation decisions without exposing private information. A disagreement with a project claim is not, by itself, misconduct.

Raise a moderation concern with a maintainer. If it involves sensitive information, request a private contact channel before sharing it; do not post another person's private details in a public issue. Maintainers should avoid deciding complaints about their own conduct alone where another maintainer is available.
